Understanding Your Result

The calculated probability mirrors patterns recognized across clinical test subjects. A lower risk score does not eliminate the possibility of underlying cardiovascular pathology, nor does an elevated score confirm presence of occlusive disease without confirmatory catheterization, nuclear imaging, or formal clinical adjudication.
